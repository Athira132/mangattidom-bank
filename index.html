Centenary Heritage

A Century-Long Legacy of Service

Mangattidom Service Co-operative Bank has been serving the people of Mangattidom and the surrounding communities in Kannur for more than a century.

The bank began its journey in 1922 as an Aikyananaaya Sangham and became a Service Co-operative Bank in 1974.

Today, the bank continues to evolve while remaining committed to community development, financial support and cooperative values.
