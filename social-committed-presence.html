Social Committed Presence

Dedicated to community welfare, mutual assistance, and public benefit across Kannur.

Beyond Banking

Rooted in People, Sustained by Trust

True to its cooperative origin since 1922, Mangattidom Service Co-operative Bank actively participates in public welfare, community assistance, and social upliftment.

Excellence in Education

Student Recognition & Talent Felicitation

Every year, the bank honours students who achieve outstanding results in SSLC examinations and appreciates individuals demonstrating excellence and talent in different fields.

By recognizing and awarding the academic and extracurricular achievements of students from local families, the bank nurtures the next generation of leadership and community development.

Agricultural Upliftment

Supporting Agriculture & Allied Activities

The bank provides loans supporting agricultural and allied activities while encouraging thrift, self-reliance and mutual assistance.

Recognizing farming as the backbone of our rural economy, the bank facilitates cooperative assistance and access to financial resources for farmers and allied workers.

Public Welfare

Thanneer Panthal: Relief in Summer

During summer, the bank sets up a Thanneer Panthal to provide free drinking water to the public.

This thoughtful seasonal service provides cool hydration and refreshing respite to pedestrians, commuters, and daily wage workers facing the peak summer heat.

Festive Welfare

Onam Markets for the Public

Every year, the bank organises Onam markets to help ordinary people manage the burden of rising prices during the festive season.

Through direct procurement and cooperative retail arrangements, the bank ensures essential commodities are made available at fair prices during Kerala's largest celebration.
